Background

The story

It started in the late 90s with a few packs ripped at the kitchen table. Like a lot of collectors, Zack grew up with Pokemon cards — trading on the playground, hunting for holos, and chasing that feeling of pulling something rare.

After years of buying and selling through forums, local shops, and eventually online marketplaces, one thing became clear: most dealers treat cards like widgets. They don't know the difference between a shadowless Base Set and a first edition. They don't care about condition the way a collector does.

Ripping Zacks was built to be different. Every card sold here is handled with care, described accurately, and priced fairly. Every card bought is evaluated with real market knowledge — not a lowball offer from a price guide.
