The story

It started in the late 90s with a few packs ripped at the kitchen table. Like a lot of collectors, Zack grew up with trading cards — trading on the playground, hunting for holos, and chasing that feeling of pulling something rare.

After years of buying and selling through forums, local shops, and eventually online marketplaces, one thing became clear: the details matter. Knowing the difference between a shadowless Base Set and a first edition, caring about condition the way a collector does — that's what makes this hobby worth doing right.

Descent TCG — founded as Ripping Zacks — was built around that same care. Every card sold here is handled carefully, described accurately, and priced fairly. Every card bought is evaluated with real market knowledge — not guesswork.
